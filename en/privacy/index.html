Privacy policy

1. Controller

Larsomatic OÜ, Tornimäe tn 5, 10145 Tallinn, Estonia, represented by Lars Timmermann. E-mail: lt@larsomatic.org

2. Hosting of this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc. / Microsoft, USA). When you open a page, GitHub automatically processes server log data (including IP address, date and time, page requested, browser type). Details: GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Fonts

The fonts on this website are served locally from this server. There is no connection to Google servers.

4. Usage measurement

We measure use of this website without cookies and without storing information on your device. We only record the event name (for example “page view”), the page path, the page language, the referring domain and the device group (mobile or desktop). No user ID is assigned and your IP address is not stored. The data is processed on our own n8n server. If “Do Not Track” is enabled in your browser, nothing is sent. Legal basis: legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

5. Third-party services

Booking (Cal.com): booking links open Cal.com, which processes your input according to its own privacy policy. WhatsApp: links to WhatsApp open Meta's WhatsApp app. Messages exchanged there are subject to WhatsApp/Meta's privacy terms.

6. Loss check (form)

If you use the lead loss check and request the result, we collect company name, e-mail, optionally phone number, the business figures you enter and the calculated result. Purpose: contacting you about the result and our offer. Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. Processing runs through our self-hosted automation (n8n on larsomatic.org), the data is stored in our Notion workspace (Notion Labs, Inc., USA). Storage until the purpose is fulfilled or you withdraw consent.

7. The Auftragspilot app and connected AI agents

When a business uses the Auftragspilot app (https://auftragspilot-crm.vercel.app/a), the app processes on behalf of that business: contact details of its leads (name, phone number, e-mail if given), the WhatsApp and Instagram messages exchanged, qualification answers, bookings and notes. An AI model processes conversation content to answer leads and qualify them. If the business connects its own AI agent, that agent receives this data through the access the business granted. The business is the controller for its leads' data, we act as processor under the data processing agreement.

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing, data portability and to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future.
