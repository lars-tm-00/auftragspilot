Official WhatsApp Business API vs. WhatsApp Web automation: why it matters for leads

You link your WhatsApp to a tool with a QR code, and ten minutes later an AI is answering. Sounds perfect. Until the first lead from your ad arrives and the tool isn't allowed to message them.

Two ways to automate WhatsApp

WhatsApp Web via QR code: the tool logs in on your phone like WhatsApp Web on a computer. No approval from Meta, ready right away. But it is not the official interface.

Official WhatsApp Business Platform: the interface Meta offers to businesses. One-time setup via Facebook, after that you may send approved templates.

Why it makes the difference for leads

Leads from ads and forms have never written to you. Over WhatsApp Web you mostly can't message them at all, or only very few per day. One well-known provider, for example, allows 10 new WhatsApp chats per day, and only with people who wrote first or opted in (as of 24 Nov 2026, according to the provider's website). If your campaign brings 40 leads a day, 30 of them wait until tomorrow.

And waiting costs: in the study by Oldroyd and colleagues (Harvard Business Review, 2011) companies took 42 hours on average for the first reply, and 23% never replied. Those that responded within an hour were seven times as likely to have a qualifying conversation.

What the official platform makes possible

  • Templates to new contacts: every lead with consent gets a WhatsApp right away, even at night.
  • Limits that grow: new numbers start low, with good quality Meta's daily limit goes up.
  • No ban risk from unofficial automation: you use exactly the route Meta provides for businesses.

To be fair: the official platform charges a Meta fee per template, depending on country and category, and needs a one-time setup. For replying to messages customers send themselves, a simpler tool is often enough.
