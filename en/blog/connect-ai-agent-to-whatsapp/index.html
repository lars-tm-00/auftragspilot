Connect your own AI agent to WhatsApp: how it works in 2 minutes

Your AI agent writes your e-mails, summarises meetings and builds spreadsheets. But when a WhatsApp enquiry comes in at 10:14 pm, it doesn't see it.

The problem: your agent is cut off from your customers

Most businesses use an AI agent today: Claude, ChatGPT, Muse or Grokbot. Customer enquiries land somewhere else, on WhatsApp, in Instagram messages, in forms. In between sits a person who copies, checks and writes back. Usually the next morning.

What it costs

Every hour of waiting makes a lead colder. In the well-known study by Oldroyd and colleagues (Harvard Business Review, 2011), firms that responded within an hour were seven times as likely to have a qualifying conversation as firms that responded an hour later. Those that waited 24 hours or longer were more than 60 times behind. Your agent could answer in seconds. It just isn't allowed to.

The fix: one sentence, the agent does the rest

Auftragspilot connects your agent to your WhatsApp and Instagram. You paste one sentence into the chat with your agent:

Set up Auftragspilot for me by following https://larsomatic.org/auftragspilot/en/agents/

The agent reads the guide, creates your account and asks you a few things about your business. You only confirm a 6-digit code from the e-mail and connect your WhatsApp number with one click via Facebook. From then on your agent reads the conversations, answers questions and books appointments into your calendar.

Prefer to do it yourself? In Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor you just add the address https://auftragspilot-crm.vercel.app/api/mcp and sign in in the browser. No key to copy.

What it looks like in practice

  • A lead fills in your Facebook ad and gets a WhatsApp with their name seconds later.
  • Your agent sees the reply, asks about budget and timing and suggests three free slots.
  • All you get is the message: “Appointment booked, Thursday 10 am.”

Auftragspilot keeps to WhatsApp's rules: consent, the 24-hour window, opt-outs. Whenever something is stopped, your agent gets a clear error code instead of a blocked number. Details on the limits page.
