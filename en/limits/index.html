Limits

Clear limits that keep your number healthy.

These limits protect your WhatsApp number and your contacts. Whenever something is stopped, your agent gets a clear error code and knows what to do.

AreaLimit
API600 requests per hour per key
Setup server (no key)300 requests per hour per IP address
Free-text repliesonly within 24 hours after the customer's last message (Meta rule), after that only approved templates
New conversations (start_conversation)60 per hour per account, consent required
Broadcastsapproved template only, only to contacts with consent (form, ad, test page or wrote first), not opted out, confirmation required, max 1,000 recipients per broadcast, 30 per minute per number, only 08:00 to 20:00
Meta's daily limitapplies on top per number: new numbers start low and grow with good quality
Scheduled messagesup to 90 days ahead
Sequencesmax 6 steps, at least 1 hour apart, stop automatically on a reply or opt-out, only 08:00 to 20:00
Idempotency keysremembered for 24 hours
One-click setup linksvalid for 2 hours
E-mail setup codevalid for 30 minutes, max 5 tries

Error codes

Every error has a code field. RATE_LIMITED also includes retry_after_seconds.

CodeMeaning
UNAUTHORIZEDMissing or invalid key or sign-in.
FORBIDDEN_SCOPEThe key lacks the permission (e.g. read only).
NOT_FOUNDContact, client or item not found.
INVALID_ARGUMENTA value is missing or has the wrong format.
RATE_LIMITEDToo many requests. Wait retry_after_seconds, then retry.
OUTSIDE_24H_WINDOWFree text only within 24 hours after the customer's last message. After that only with an approved template.
CONSENT_REQUIREDThere is no consent for this contact.
OPTED_OUTThe contact opted out (STOP).
HANDED_OVERA person has taken over the conversation.
NOT_CONNECTEDNo WhatsApp number or channel connected.
TEMPLATE_REQUIREDAn approved template is required, e.g. outside the 24-hour window or for broadcasts.
NEEDS_CONFIRMATIONThe action needs explicit confirmation (e.g. sending a broadcast).
OUTSIDE_SENDING_HOURSOutside 08:00 to 20:00 in the account's time zone.
NEEDS_APPROVALDraft key: the message was saved for approval, not sent.
PLAN_REQUIREDThis needs an active plan.
