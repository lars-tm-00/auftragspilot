Method

How this number is calculated

The calculator uses only your own inputs and shows scenarios. It makes no forecast and uses no invented industry values.

  1. 1

    Your own numbers. Inquiries per month, your closing rate and the average job value come directly from you. From these we calculate your current monthly revenue from inquiries (inquiries × closing rate × job value).

  2. 2

    Value of an inquiry. Closing rate × job value. At 20 % and €30,000, an inquiry is worth €6,000 to you on average.

  3. 3

    Scenarios instead of a forecast. We show what it means if late replies cost you 5, 10 or 20 % of your inquiries. These shares are worked examples, not study results. How many inquiries it really is for you is best seen in your own data.

Why speed matters, source: Oldroyd, J. B., McElheran, K., & Elkington, D. (2011).The Short Life of Online Sales Leads. Harvard Business Review, 89(3), p. 28. Checked against the original article. In a sub-study of 1.25 million leads at 42 US companies, the chance of a qualifying conversation with a decision maker was about 7 times higher when first contact happened within 1 hour instead of one hour later. Compared with firms that waited 24 hours or longer, the factor was over 60. Important: the study measures the chance of a qualifying conversation, not of a closing. That is why this calculator does not compute with the factor and shows scenarios only.

Fair note: The study is American and cross-industry, not collected specifically for the DACH market or a single industry. The scenarios are not a promise for your business.
