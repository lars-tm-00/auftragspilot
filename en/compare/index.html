When Spaces fits better

If you mainly need LinkedIn, Messenger or TikTok, Spaces covers more channels than we do today. If you only want to answer incoming messages and already have your own agent, Spaces works well too.

When Auftragspilot fits better

If your leads come from ads and forms and should get a WhatsApp right away. That only works with templates over the official WhatsApp Business Platform. Add the built-in AI that books appointments, and the option that we set everything up for you.

Official API or WhatsApp Web automation?

Many tools link WhatsApp by QR code, like WhatsApp Web on a computer. It's quick and needs no approval from Meta, but it is not the official interface. The result: no templates, low limits for new chats and a risk that the number gets restricted when messages are sent automatically.

The official WhatsApp Business Platform needs a one-time setup via Facebook. After that you may send approved templates to contacts with consent, even if they have never written, with limits that grow with good quality.
