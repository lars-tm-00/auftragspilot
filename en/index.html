Without a system

The gap is not just the response time.

An analysis of 60 high-ticket companies shows: within opening hours many still respond acceptably. Outside of them, and at the appointment booking itself, it looks different.

0/ 60

companies examined offer direct online appointment booking. The prospect has to wait for a call back.

  • Dead outside opening hours

    Whoever inquires in the evening or at the weekend often waits until the next working day. In the meantime they have usually already asked the competition.

  • Manual quotes

    Staff combine customer data, product parameters and pricing rules by hand before a first quote goes out.

  • Qualifying takes time

    Every inquiry first has to be sorted manually before it is clear whether it is worth pursuing at all.

  • Every gap costs jobs

    Whoever offers an appointment first wins the job, regardless of how good the answer itself was.

Industries

Wherever an inquiry first needs advice.

Auftragspilot is built for high-ticket products: wherever an inquiry has to be advised, qualified or calculated before an appointment happens.

Real estate

Agents, developers, project developers+

A prospect sees an ad for a new-build project and writes via WhatsApp or the brochure form. Auftragspilot asks for budget, preferred location and financing status, sends the matching brochure automatically and books the viewing straight into the agent’s calendar.

Construction & building services

Heat pumps, stairlifts, windows, renovation+

A prospect inquires via a heat pump or stairlift form. Auftragspilot clarifies the property details (house type, existing heating system or staircase shape), creates a first cost estimate and directly proposes an appointment for the on-site survey.

Mobility & vehicles

Vehicle sales, mobility aids, custom builds+

A prospect asks about a vehicle or a mobility aid, such as a wheelchair ramp or a vehicle conversion. Auftragspilot clarifies requirements and budget, sends a matching quote and books a test drive or consultation directly.

Advisory & finance

Lawyers, tax advisors, financial and insurance brokers+

A client asks for an initial consultation via the website or a referral. Auftragspilot clarifies the matter (such as area of law or type of financing), checks the rough fit and books a first appointment straight into the advisor’s calendar.

B2B equipment & industry

Machinery, plants, industrial suppliers+

A buyer inquires about a machine or plant. Auftragspilot captures the technical key data (quantity, specification, delivery location), creates a first rough calculation and arranges a technical conversation with sales.
